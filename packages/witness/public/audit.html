Every release of a protected CI secret — and every refused attempt — is published to a Hedera Consensus Service topic before the secret moves. This page reads that topic directly from the public mirror node, in your browser. It talks to no KLAXON server. For each entry it also fetches the payment and checks, here, that the transaction memo really is the commitment hash the runner signed.

What a green check means. The runner signs a commitment naming the secret, the environment and the run, hashes it, and pays the witness with that hash as the transaction memo. The witness cannot forge the record, because it needs GitHub's signature and the runner's; it cannot suppress it, because the payment is on a ledger it does not control; and it cannot back-date it, because Hedera orders it. The check above is that comparison, recomputed from public data every time this page loads.
